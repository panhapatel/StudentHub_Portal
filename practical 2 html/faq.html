Frequently Asked Questions

Home | About | Register | Login | Contact Us | FAQ

1. What is StudentHub Portal?

StudentHub Portal is an online platform that helps students manage their academic information and activities.

2. How can I register on StudentHub?

Click on the Register option and fill in the required details. Then click on the Register button.

3. How can I login?

If you already have an account, click on Login and enter your username and password.

4. What should I do if I have not registered?

Click on Create an Account to register first.

5. What information can I access through StudentHub?

Students can access their profile, attendance, results, notices, events and other academic information.

6. How can I contact StudentHub?

You can visit the Contact Us page and send us a message.

7. What should I do if I have a problem with the portal?

You can contact the StudentHub support team through the Contact Us page.
